## Name
Smooth Chase Cam

## Summary (one line, shown on the card)
A smooth, adjustable chase camera for any vehicle, started from the vehicle's own window.

## Description

Smooth Chase Cam puts a camera right behind any vehicle you select and keeps it there, turning gently with the vehicle instead of snapping round every corner.

**How to use it**
1. Click any of your vehicles to open its window.
2. Find the **Advanced Chase Control** card and press **Chase camera**.
3. The button turns green: the camera is on. Press it again to stop and get the normal game camera back.
4. Unfold **Advanced chase control** to change the view with sliders.

**What you can adjust**
- **Looseness** - how much the camera lags behind the vehicle in turns. Low is tight, high is relaxed and swingy. Shown as a percentage and in seconds.
- **Distance** - how far behind the vehicle the camera sits. This is how you zoom: the mouse wheel does not work while the chase camera is on.
- **Pitch** - from almost level with the vehicle to looking down on it.
- **Angle** - move the camera round to either side of "straight behind".

**Nice touches**
- Orbit with the middle mouse button while the camera is on, and your new view is saved as your chase view.
- One shared setting for every vehicle, saved between sessions. **Reset** returns to the defaults.
- The panel tells you when the camera is on, and says why if it switched itself off (for example when you moved the camera away or pressed Esc).
- Your open/closed choice for the settings section is remembered too.

**Good to know**
- It uses the game's own follow camera, so the closest it can get is about 16 m and the lowest pitch is 2 degrees. These are game limits.
- It only touches the camera and the vehicle window. No gameplay, economy or save-game changes, and the mod is marked cosmetic so achievements still work.
- Another mod that also controls the camera may override it while both are active.
- Tested on road vehicles and trains. Ships, aircraft and helicopters use the same code and should work, but have had less testing - tell me if one misbehaves.
- Everything is in English.
