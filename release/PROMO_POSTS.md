## 1. Urban Games forum - "Mods and Modding" board (board description says promotions are welcome)
Title: Smooth Chase Cam - a chase camera for any vehicle, with live sliders

Hi all,

I've just released a small script mod called Smooth Chase Cam. It adds a card to the vehicle window with a "Chase camera" button. Press it and the camera sits behind the vehicle and turns gently with it, instead of snapping round every corner.

What you can set (all saved between sessions): looseness (how much the camera lags in turns), distance, pitch and angle. The button turns green while it's on, and the card tells you why if it switches itself off. Orbiting with the middle mouse button while it's on gets saved as your new view.

Honest limits: the game's follow camera won't go closer than about 16 m or flatter than 2 degrees, and the mouse wheel doesn't zoom while it's on (use the Distance slider). I've tested it on road vehicles and trains; ships and aircraft use the same code but have had less testing, so reports are very welcome. It's marked cosmetic, so achievements still work.

Link: https://mod.io/g/transportfever3/m/smooth-chase-cam

Feedback and bug reports are welcome. If something misbehaves, the lines starting with [VehicleCams] in your stdout.txt help a lot.
